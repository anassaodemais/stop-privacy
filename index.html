Privacy Policy

Stop! A Little Wordplay. Last updated 19 November 2026.

  • No account, no tracking, no ads. We don't collect your name, email, location or contacts, and we don't use analytics or advertising tools.
  • Your rounds stay on your phone. Answers, scores, history and settings are saved only on your device. Deleting the app deletes them.
  • Playing together and answer checks. When you play with someone on another phone, or when the app checks if an answer is a real word, the game sends the round (letter, categories, answers and the nickname you type) to our game server. It is used only to run the game and is deleted after the game ends.
  • Stop! Plus. Payments are handled by Apple or Google. We never see your card or payment details. You can cancel anytime in your App Store or Google Play account settings.
  • Children. The app doesn't knowingly collect personal data from anyone.
  • Questions. Use the support link on the app's store page.
